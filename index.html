Chess Link ♞

Two phones, no signal needed. Moves pass between phones as QR codes.

Your name (shown to your rival, used in saved games)

How it works
  1. One player taps New match and shows the invite code. The other player taps Join and scans it.
  2. After each move, the mover shows a QR code on screen. The other player scans it (live camera, or take a photo of it) and then makes their reply.
  3. Every code holds the whole game, so a missed or repeated scan does no harm, and you can stop and carry on hours or days later.
  4. Clocks (if used) only run while it is your turn to think. Time spent showing and scanning is free.

Hotspot link

1. Turn on a hotspot on one phone (mobile data can stay off) and join the other phone to it.
2. Turn the VPN off on both phones.
3. Allow the camera when asked.
4. The first phone makes a code, the other scans it and shows its reply, and the first scans that. You only do this once; after that moves send themselves.
